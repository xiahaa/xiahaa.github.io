Dr. Xiao Hu is a researcher and engineering leader working at the intersection of the low-altitude economy, UAV autonomy, and intelligent transportation. He currently leads a team of 10+ researchers at the Lower Airspace Economy Research Institute of the International Digital Economy Academy (IDEA), developing the Smart Integrated Lower Airspace System (SILAS) and conducting research on city-scale reconstruction and multi-agent planning. He also serves as an Adjunct Assistant Professor at HKUST (Guangzhou). He received his Ph.D. from the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) with a thesis on vision-based positioning for UAVs (GPA 12/12), and brings more than 12 years of academic and industrial R&D experience across IDEA, HKUST(GZ), Alibaba DAMO Academy, TrackMan, DJI, and DTU. His research spans low-altitude spatial infrastructure, perception and localization, multi-sensor fusion, autonomous decision making, motion and mission planning, and applied AI. Algorithms he developed have been deployed in DJI commercial UAVs, including the Phantom 4, Mavic 2, and Inspire 2.
